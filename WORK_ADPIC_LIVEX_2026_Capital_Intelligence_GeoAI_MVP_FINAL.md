# WORK - ADPIC LIVEX 2026 --- Capital Intelligence GeoAI MVP

## Objective

-   WORK-OBJ-1: Deliver an exhibition-ready Capital Intelligence GeoAI
    MVP for LIVEX 2026 that demonstrates the decision-support story
    **Understand the place → Evaluate an investment → Simulate its
    impact** across the approved Abu Dhabi Areas of Interest.
-   WORK-OBJ-2: Demonstrate exactly three core capabilities: **Explore
    the Capital & Liveability Landscape**, **AI-Assisted Capital
    Investment Assessment**, and **Liveability Impact Simulator**.
-   WORK-OBJ-3: Preserve the computation and authority boundary **GIS
    computes. Business rules score. AI explains.**
-   WORK-OBJ-4: Present the solution as a compact, future-facing Capital
    Intelligence Twin for planning and decision support rather than a
    traditional GIS dashboard.
-   WORK-OBJ-5: Complete the curated presenter journey in 60--90 seconds
    under exhibition conditions without making AI an approval authority.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   WORK-AC-1: The application exposes only the three MVP capabilities
    and the presenter can complete the narrative **Understand → Evaluate
    → Simulate** in 60--90 seconds.
-   WORK-AC-2: Al Reem Island can open as the Explore/3D starting AOI
    when its 3D technical spike passes; Khalifa City is available for
    Evaluate and Simulate.
-   WORK-AC-3: AOI switching between included AOIs occurs without a full
    application reload and preserves the core workflow.
-   WORK-AC-4: The exhibition experience retains validated portfolio KPI
    counts while presenting at least 20 representative curated showcase
    projects per included AOI; the target curation range is 20--40 per
    AOI.
-   WORK-AC-5: A presenter can select a planned/proposed project and
    receive an assessment generated by deterministic GIS indicators and
    configured business rules.
-   WORK-AC-6: Every displayed assessment result exposes sufficient
    evidence to explain why the result was produced and is traceable to
    configured inputs/rules.
-   WORK-AC-7: The assessment uses owner-approved **Low / Medium /
    High** exhibition-only priority output and visibly identifies it as
    illustrative decision support, not official approval or an
    investment decision.
-   WORK-AC-8: The simulator can demonstrate the approved hypothetical
    **school** scenario using approved real context and compare
    **Current** versus **With Proposed Project**.
-   WORK-AC-9: Simulation displays spatial before/after evidence
    including current coverage, underserved areas, proposed location,
    new service area, newly covered communities where supported by
    approved data, and corresponding KPI changes.
-   WORK-AC-10: AI explanations are grounded only in approved/sanitized
    structured context supplied by the application and never invent
    unavailable project facts or unsupported numbers.
-   WORK-AC-11: When requested information is absent from the approved
    demo context, AI/template output states that the information is not
    included in the demo dataset rather than guessing.
-   WORK-AC-12: The full scripted core demo remains operable without a
    live LLM by using deterministic/pre-authored explanation templates.
-   WORK-AC-13: No confidential or unapproved information is exposed in
    the exhibition build.
-   WORK-AC-14: The primary exhibition experience is usable on the
    validated presenter-operated large-display/touchscreen setup and is
    verified on the actual event hardware before exhibition-build
    commitment.
-   WORK-AC-15: Critical presenter actions are stable without manual
    data editing during the demo.
-   WORK-AC-16: A fixed deterministic fallback demonstration and backup
    video are prepared and verified before go-live.
-   WORK-AC-17: The solution architecture is English UI with
    Arabic-ready architecture; content/layout architecture must not
    prevent later Arabic localization.
-   WORK-AC-18: External Esri 3D/basemap/service dependencies required
    by the final exhibition path pass the technical spike or the
    approved fallback path is used.
-   WORK-AC-19: The full portfolio source counts displayed to users are
    frozen/validated event-snapshot values; unvalidated 219/139 counts
    are not presented as authoritative event facts.
-   WORK-AC-20: All unresolved EPIC_CREATE open questions in this
    contract are resolved with provenance before `ai work` is allowed to
    proceed to final epic creation.

## Business Rules

-   WORK-BR-1: The invariant computation boundary is **GIS computes;
    business rules score; AI explains**.
-   WORK-BR-2: AI must never approve, reject, authorize, or make an
    official capital-investment/project-prioritization decision.
-   WORK-BR-3: Assessment scoring must be deterministic and reproducible
    from the same approved inputs and configured rules.
-   WORK-BR-4: The assessment dimensions may include Strategic
    Alignment, Community Need, Spatial Service Gap, Existing Project
    Duplication, Accessibility Benefit, Infrastructure Dependency,
    Delivery Complexity, and Overall Priority where the approved
    dataset/rules support them.
-   WORK-BR-5: Community Need may be derived from configured
    population/demand, growth, existing-service, distance, and
    underserved-population inputs.
-   WORK-BR-6: Strategic Alignment must use configured/approved themes
    or preclassified relationships; automated interpretation of
    unrestricted strategy documents is not required.
-   WORK-BR-7: Spatial Service Gap may use population outside service
    areas, distance to service, future demand, and spatial distribution
    where approved data exists.
-   WORK-BR-8: Duplication analysis must compare similar
    active/planned/approved projects within a configured radius or
    catchment.
-   WORK-BR-9: Any scoring weights remain configurable and must not be
    represented as an official ADPIC methodology unless explicitly
    validated and approved.
-   WORK-BR-10: The source's illustrative weights (Need 30, Strategic
    25, Gap 25, Accessibility 10, Duplication penalty 5,
    Delivery/dependency penalty 5) are examples only and are not
    automatically the exhibition methodology.
-   WORK-BR-11: Full portfolio records may support KPI/analytical
    queries where performance allows, but not every record must receive
    equal 3D visual prominence.
-   WORK-BR-12: Representative/synthetic demo records may be used only
    where needed and must be internally identifiable as demo data.
-   WORK-BR-13: Yas Island is fallback scope only and must not become a
    third core AOI or additional feature scope.
-   WORK-BR-14: The school simulation is hypothetical and uses approved
    real context; it must not be presented as an approved real capital
    project.
-   WORK-BR-15: The assessment must display the owner-approved wording
    **"Illustrative decision-support assessment for demonstration
    purposes; not an official project approval or investment
    decision."** unless an authorized communications/legal owner
    supplies replacement wording before exhibition release.

## Functional Requirements

-   WORK-REQ-1: Provide a 3D Explore experience showing community
    context, approved/sanitized capital projects, relevant
    status/type/budget/timeline/liveability attributes, and major
    facilities/infrastructure supported by the event dataset.
-   WORK-REQ-2: Allow a presenter to select a project in the 3D
    experience and open a concise project card using approved
    attributes.
-   WORK-REQ-3: Provide a compact KPI strip capable of representing
    approved portfolio indicators such as total projects, total capital
    investment, under delivery, planned, population served, and
    liveability themes when those fields are approved and available.
-   WORK-REQ-4: Provide project filtering appropriate to the approved
    dataset, with source examples including Mobility, Education, Health,
    Public Realm, Community Facilities, and Utilities/Infrastructure.
-   WORK-REQ-5: Support cinematic fly-to/highlighting, 3D context,
    project emphasis, status visualization and scale-aware decluttering
    appropriate for an exhibition experience.
-   WORK-REQ-6: Provide an AOI selector supporting Khalifa City and,
    conditional on successful technical validation, Al Reem Island
    without changing the core workflow.
-   WORK-REQ-7: Use Al Reem Island as the preferred opening Explore/3D
    story when its 3D spike succeeds.
-   WORK-REQ-8: Use Khalifa City as the mandatory Evaluate and Simulate
    story.
-   WORK-REQ-9: Provide an AI-Assisted Capital Investment Assessment for
    a selected proposed/planned project answering the decision-support
    question **"Should this project be prioritised?"** without turning
    the answer into an approval.
-   WORK-REQ-10: Show the evidence/indicators contributing to the
    assessment, not only the resulting category.
-   WORK-REQ-11: Produce the approved exhibition-only Low/Medium/High
    assessment using deterministic GIS/business rules.
-   WORK-REQ-12: Provide a Liveability Impact Simulator comparing
    **Current** and **With Proposed Project** for the approved
    hypothetical school scenario.
-   WORK-REQ-13: The simulator must be capable of showing population
    within service area, service coverage, average access distance,
    underserved population, and a Liveability Impact Score when
    supported by approved data/rules.
-   WORK-REQ-14: The simulation map must be capable of showing current
    coverage, underserved areas, proposed location, new service area,
    and newly covered communities when supported by approved data.
-   WORK-REQ-15: Simulation interaction must support the exhibition
    sequence: current state → Simulate → proposed 3D intervention →
    expanded/changed service coverage → KPI change → concise
    explanation.
-   WORK-REQ-16: Provide **Ask ADPIC AI** as a cross-cutting contextual
    explanation interaction rather than a fourth standalone module.
-   WORK-REQ-17: AI/explanation context must be assembled by the
    application from selected project, current map context, approved
    project attributes, calculated GIS indicators, and configured
    strategy information.
-   WORK-REQ-18: AI interaction must favor suggested questions and
    allowlisted map actions rather than unrestricted autonomous chat.
-   WORK-REQ-19: AI responses may include a concise explanation plus
    structured/allowlisted map action such as project focus or layer
    visibility.
-   WORK-REQ-20: Provide deterministic/pre-authored explanation
    templates sufficient to execute the entire scripted demo when live
    LLM access is unavailable or unapproved.
-   WORK-REQ-21: Keep the MVP to a compact screen flow: Hero/Explore →
    Evaluate/Assessment → Simulate, avoiding unnecessary pages.
-   WORK-REQ-22: Provide a curated presenter journey matching the source
    timing: context, Reem Explore when available, Khalifa Evaluate,
    Khalifa Simulate, closing impact statement.
-   WORK-REQ-23: Support a fixed deterministic demo path suitable for
    exhibition recovery/fallback.
-   WORK-REQ-24: Preserve Yas Island as a replacement AOI option only if
    required by 3D quality, data permissions, or story viability.

## Security / Compliance

-   WORK-SEC-1: Only approved/sanitized event data may be exposed
    publicly.
-   WORK-SEC-2: The exact public project-attribute allowlist requires
    explicit approval before epic creation/final data preparation.
-   WORK-SEC-3: AI/template explanations must not invent project facts,
    numbers, approvals, or unavailable information.
-   WORK-SEC-4: Live LLM integration, if later enabled, must receive
    only explicitly approved structured/sanitized context.
-   WORK-SEC-5: No unrestricted confidential datasets or documents may
    be supplied to a public/unapproved AI service.
-   WORK-SEC-6: Representative/demo records must be labeled/managed so
    they cannot be mistaken internally for verified production records.
-   WORK-SEC-7: Assessment output must visibly remain decision support
    and not an official approval/investment decision.
-   WORK-SEC-8: External services, credentials, API keys/OAuth and
    credits must follow the approved exhibition security configuration.
-   WORK-SEC-9: The exhibition build must not contain confidential
    information.
